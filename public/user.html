Help & Support

If you're facing threats, harassment, or cyberbullying:

  • Contact the DCI Cybercrime Unit for digital evidence issues.
  • Reach out to your nearest Police Station if safety is at risk.
  • Email complaints@ca.go.ke for online harassment cases.
  • For legal or emotional support, contact legalhelp@example.org.
