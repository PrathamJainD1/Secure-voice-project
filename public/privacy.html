Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 6, 2025

At SafeReport, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Report details you choose to submit
  • Account information (if you create an account)
  • Technical data for security and service improvement

2. Anonymous Reporting

When you choose to submit an anonymous report, we do not collect any personally identifiable information. Your identity remains completely protected.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption
  • Secure data storage
  • Regular security audits
  • Limited access to authorized personnel only

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Processing and reviewing your reports
  • Communicating with you about your reports
  • Improving our services
  • Complying with legal obligations

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request data deletion
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
