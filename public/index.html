How It Works

1

Submit Your Report

Fill out a secure form describing the incident. Include any relevant evidence such as screenshots, documents, or photos.

2

Receive Case ID

You'll immediately receive a unique case ID to track your report's progress and receive updates.

3

Professional Review

Our support team reviews your case promptly and determines the appropriate action while keeping you informed.

4

Get Support

Access counseling resources, legal information, and ongoing support throughout the resolution process.
