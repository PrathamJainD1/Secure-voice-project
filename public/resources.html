Understanding Your Rights

You have the right to report incidents without fear of retaliation. Our platform ensures your identity remains protected throughout the process.

  • Right to anonymity
  • Right to confidentiality
  • Right to follow up on your report
  • Right to support and resources

FAQs

How anonymous is my report?

Your report is completely anonymous if you choose that option. We do not collect any identifying information.

How long does the review process take?

Most reports are reviewed within 48-72 hours. Complex cases may take longer.

Can I update my report after submission?

Yes, you can add updates or additional information through your dashboard.
